Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

1-Click Ask Summarizer for YouTube™ ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Extension operates entirely within your local web browser:

  • No Personal Data Collected: The Extension does not collect, record, transmit, or monetize any personal information, browsing history, video viewing history, or queries.
  • No External Servers: The Extension connects to zero external servers, third-party analytics, or telemetry APIs. All actions execute directly and locally in your browser.
  • No Account Needed: You do not need an account, sign-in, or API keys to use the extension.

2. Permissions Justification

  • storage: Stores your local interface preferences (e.g. enabling or disabling the on-video button). No tracking or analytics data is stored.
  • activeTab: Connects to the active YouTube tab when you click the extension popup or use the keyboard shortcut (Alt+S / ⌥+S).
  • host_permissions (youtube.com): Required to detect YouTube's native experimental "Ask about this video" feature and display the 1-click summarize overlay button on the player.

3. Third-Party Services

The Extension automates interaction with YouTube's built-in "Ask about this video" feature provided by Google / YouTube. Any interaction with YouTube's AI feature is governed by YouTube's standard Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Chrome Web Store extension support page.
