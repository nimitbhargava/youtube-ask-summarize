# Privacy Policy for 1-Click Ask Summarizer for YouTube™

Last updated: September 4, 2026

**1-Click Ask Summarizer for YouTube™** ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our data handling practices.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
The Extension operates entirely within your local browser:
- **No Personal Data Collected**: The Extension does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, video viewing history, search queries, or user identifiers.
- **No External Servers**: The Extension does not communicate with any external servers, third-party analytics services, or remote APIs. All automation occurs natively and locally on `youtube.com` within your own browser session.
- **No Account Needed**: You do not need to register, create an account, or enter any API keys to use the Extension.

---

### 2. Permissions Justification
The Extension requests the minimum necessary Chrome permissions to perform its single purpose:
- **`storage`**: Used solely to persist your local user preferences (such as enabling/disabling the on-video overlay button or auto-summarize mode) across browser sessions. No user history or tracking data is stored.
- **`activeTab`**: Used to communicate with the currently active YouTube tab when you click the extension popup icon or trigger the keyboard shortcut (`Alt+S` / `⌥+S`).
- **`host_permissions` (`https://www.youtube.com/*`, `https://m.youtube.com/*`)**: Required to detect YouTube's native experimental "Ask about this video" feature and inject the 1-click summarize overlay button onto the video player.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services
The Extension automates interaction with YouTube's built-in "Ask about this video" (Gemini) feature provided directly by Google / YouTube. Any interaction with YouTube's AI feature is governed by YouTube's and Google's standard Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. The Extension itself does not proxy, intercept, or store any prompts or responses.

---

### 4. Changes to This Policy
If this Privacy Policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

---

### 5. Contact
If you have questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support page.
